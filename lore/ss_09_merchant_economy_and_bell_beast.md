# Lore Thread 09 (Silksong): The Guild and Merchant Economy and the Bell Beast

- **Topic:** Commercial Guilds, Transit Infrastructure, and the Bell Beast
- **Game:** Hollow Knight: Silksong
- **Source Basis:** Secondary community transcriptions (`src_wiki_ss_areas`, `src_wiki_ss_characters`, `src_wiki_ss_bosses`), developer interviews, and economic game studies.
- **Spoilers:** Details of the Bell Beast boss encounter, Gilded City bazaars, and transit networks.

## 1. What the Games and Developers Say (Tier 1 Canon)
The economy of Pharloom operates through a sophisticated commercial network centered around the **Gilded City** and its regional guild outposts [CLM-SS09-001]. Currency is denominated in **Rosary Beads**, perforated prayer beads that are strung on sturdy thread and spent at merchant stalls or converted into lethal projectile ammunition [CLM-SS09-002]. When dying in combat, Hornet drops her unstrung Rosaries, which can be protected by stringing them onto rosary cords at benches [CLM-SS09-003].

In the lower industrial districts of The Marrow, Hornet encounters the **Bell Beast**, an enormous, multi-legged armored transport behemoth [CLM-SS09-004]. Enslaved by the theocracy through heavy ringing bronze bells clamped around its neck, the Bell Beast attacks Hornet in a frenzied, disoriented rage [CLM-SS09-005]. Upon defeating the beast and severing its acoustic collar, the creature recognizes Hornet as an ally, transforming into Pharloom's fast-travel transit system, carrying Hornet across subterranean bellways in place of the Stags of Hallownest [CLM-SS09-006].

In the Gilded City, wealthy traders like the **Gilded Merchant** and artisan crafters exchange rare needle alloys, pins, and shell shards for heavy purses of Rosaries [CLM-SS09-007]. Artisan blacksmiths like the **Forge-Daughter** in Deep Docks and the **Pinstress** in Bonebottom provide weapon refinement and tool customization, demonstrating a thriving culture of independent craft [CLM-SS09-008].

## 2. What the Evidence Strongly Implies (Tier 2 Interpretation)
The Bell Beast transit network reveals that Pharloom's industrialization was achieved through the brutal subjugation of native megafauna: gentle transport creatures were violently bound with ringing bells, converting living beasts into industrialized transit machinery [CLM-SS09-009]. The monetization of Rosaries underscores the seamless fusion between religious liturgy and commercial capitalism: prayer is literal currency, and the church operates simultaneously as a state bank and judicial enforcement agency [CLM-SS09-010]. By liberating the Bell Beast, Hornet dismantles one of the theocracy's primary instruments of forced servitude, substituting violent acoustic coercion with mutual interspecies respect [CLM-SS09-011].

## 3. Interpretations and Gaps (Tier 3 and Disputed)
Scholars debate whether the Bell Beast is the last surviving member of an ancient subterranean species or if wild herds still roam the untamed outer crags of Pharloom [CLM-SS09-012].

## 4. Why It Matters for the Project's Themes
The merchant economy and the Bell Beast illustrate that Pharloom is a living, functional society rather than a long-dead ruin. Unlike Hallownest's lonely, abandoned Stag stations where only the Last Stag remained, Pharloom presents an active struggle between commercial exploitation and grassroots resistance.
