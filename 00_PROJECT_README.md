## 3. Epistemological and Canon Boundaries

To preserve academic integrity, all narrative, historical, and ludic evidence is categorized into three strict epistemic tiers:
1. **Confirmed Canon (Tier 1):** Directly expressed in in-game text, dialogue, official instruction material, or explicit developer statements (for example: Team Cherry blogs, official developer retrospectives).
2. **Strongly Supported Interpretation (Tier 2):** Inferences derived from converging, independent environmental, mechanical, and textual clues without direct contradiction.
3. **Speculative Theory (Tier 3):** Plausible hypotheses addressing unresolved ambiguities (such as the precise chronological duration between games or cosmic origins of the Void). Speculative theories are explicitly designated and never presented as confirmed fact.
