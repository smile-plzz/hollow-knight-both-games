## 1. Reviewer Reports

### Reviewer 1: Game Studies & Ludology
- **Evaluation of Core Contributions:** This manuscript provides an exemplary contribution to the ludological and spatial analysis of the Metroidvania genre. The authors avoid the common trap of treating game environments as passive backdrops for platforming mechanics; instead, they rigorously demonstrate how the spatial topology—specifically the subterranean descent of Hallownest versus the vertical ascent of Pharloom—operates as an ideological and narrative delivery mechanism. The analysis of Bohan Zhang’s (2026) backtracking narrative mechanism is particularly illuminating, convincingly contrasting Hallownest’s archaeological excavation with *Silksong*’s delivery-based communal repair.
- **Methodological Strengths:**
  - Precise comparative frame analysis between Nail and Needle combat, highlighting how directional pogoing vs. 45-degree diagonal lunges alters the player's kinetic posture.
  - Excellent deconstruction of the resource economies: Soul-Focus (vulnerable, stationary meditation) vs. Silk-Bind (rapid, mid-air athletic triage).
  - Clear integration of Ian Bogost’s procedural rhetoric and Espen Aarseth’s ergodic traversal.
- **Constructive Critiques & Required Minor Revisions:**
  - *Critique 1:* The discussion of the Crest and Tool system in *Silksong* is mechanically sound, but could further analyze how the crafting economy (Rosaries and Shell Shards) alters player risk perception compared to the traditional "corpse run" mechanic of *Hollow Knight*'s Shade.
  - *Critique 2:* Ensure that the mechanical differences in boss AI aggression between the two games are tied back to the player's expanded movement suite.

---

### Reviewer 2: Environmental Humanities & Ecocriticism
- **Evaluation of Core Contributions:** The ecological analysis is one of the strongest pillars of this manuscript. Grounding the civilizational decay of Hallownest in Timothy Morton’s "dark ecology" and Alenda Chang’s non-anthropocentric game design offers a profound corrective to human-centric readings of video game worlds. The authors effectively show that Hallownest’s Infection is not an alien biological blight invading a pristine paradise, but the violent return of an ancient, suppressed solar ecosystem resisting royal stone monuments.
- **Methodological Strengths:**
  - Masterful application of Donna Haraway’s *Staying with the Trouble* (2016) to the Weavers of Deepnest and Pharloom, framing silk as a sympoietic, multi-species craft of survival.
  - Authentic integration of entomological science (Wilson & Hölldobler’s eusocial superorganisms) to evaluate the Hive and Pharloom’s labor castes.
  - Insightful treatment of fungal networks (Mister Mushroom) as symbols of deep-time biological indifference outlasting divine kings.
- **Constructive Critiques & Required Minor Revisions:**
  - *Critique 1:* While the comparison of the Radiance to *Ophiocordyceps* parasitism is persuasive, the manuscript should explicitly remind the reader that fictional game parasites combine psychic and dream-realm elements that have no literal biological equivalent.
  - *Critique 2:* Elaborate slightly on the ecological role of Bilewater in Pharloom as an example of industrial environmental degradation within an arthropod society.

---

### Reviewer 3: Philosophy, Ethics, and Religious Studies
- **Evaluation of Core Contributions:** The paper's engagement with moral philosophy and political theology is exceptionally sophisticated. Utilizing Alex Grunberg’s (2024) Omelas framework alongside Giorgio Agamben’s (1998) *homo sacer* provides a devastating, academically rigorous indictment of the Pale King’s eugenic regime. The classification of the Vessels as "bare life" produced by sovereign exception is a brilliant theoretical contribution to video game ethics.
- **Methodological Strengths:**
  - Compelling analysis of the six *Hollow Knight* endings, demonstrating how procedural interactivity allows players to move beyond Le Guin's passive "walking away" into active systemic transformation or deicide.
  - Nuanced treatment of Jan Wysocki’s (2026) review of *Silksong*, examining how liturgical aesthetics (hymns, organ pipes, bells) convert somatic extraction into spiritual virtue.
  - Appropriate application of Rudolf Otto’s *mysterium tremendum* to explain the awe and dread inspired by ancient higher beings.
- **Constructive Critiques & Required Minor Revisions:**
  - *Critique 1:* The discussion of Ending 4 (*Embrace the Void*) should clarify whether the Void Entity represents moral liberation or merely the replacement of solar despotism with entropic annihilation.
  - *Critique 2:* Ensure the distinction between voluntary ascetic sacrifice (Quirrel, Monomon) and involuntary state sacrifice (the Vessels, the Pilgrims) remains sharply drawn throughout.

### Reviewer 4: Narrative, Character, and Cultural Studies
- **Evaluation of Core Contributions:** The character analyses in this manuscript are perceptive, comprehensive, and emotionally grounded. The authors successfully avoid flattening nonhuman characters into caricatures. The central comparative study between the Knight and Hornet—contrasting an empty, silent sacrificial instrument with a voiced, agile princess-protector carrying complex family lineage—is a masterclass in comparative character ontology.
- **Methodological Strengths:**
  - Moving and rigorous treatment of the Hollow Knight’s self-mutilation during combat as proceduralized suicidal agony.
  - Sensitive analysis of subaltern and comic figures (Myla’s tragic song, Quirrel’s peaceful departure at Blue Lake, Zote’s toxic masculine satire).
  - Rich treatment of the rivalry and burgeoning intimacy between Hornet and Lace.
- **Constructive Critiques & Required Minor Revisions:**
  - *Critique 1:* In the analysis of Hornet's parentage, make sure to emphasize that Herrah's bargain was driven by tribal pride and maternal ambition rather than political submission to the Pale King.
  - *Critique 2:* Ensure that the distinction between canon-confirmed lore and speculative community theories regarding the timeline between the two games is maintained with absolute consistency.

---

### Reviewer 5: Devil's Advocate Critique
- **Core Challenge to the Manuscript's Thesis:**
  As the designated contrarian, I must aggressively challenge the manuscript's fundamental premise: **Are we over-interpreting a pair of atmospheric indie video games?** 
  
  Does Team Cherry actually possess a grand, coherent philosophy of civilizational collapse, or did they merely assemble a collage of evocative tropes (ruined castles, glowing bugs, cute round masks, sad piano chords) that invite players to project their own literary and philosophical obsessions? 

- **Specific Counterarguments Raised:**
  1. *The Illusion of Lore Coherence:* Is the lore of Hallownest truly an integrated historical framework, or is it an "iceberg illusion" where vague, fragmented inscriptions give the false impression of an underlying structure that the developers never actually fully articulated?
  2. *The Intentionality of the Void:* Is the Knight's apparent "lack of will" a profound existential meditation on identity, or was it simply a convenient narrative excuse for why an action-game avatar never speaks and attacks whatever the player points at?
  3. *The Aestheticization of Biology:* Are the biological references (eusociality, molting, silk spinning) truly structural, or are they merely superficial visual skins draped over standard video game tropes (health potions, fast travel, grappling hooks)?
  4. *Difficulty vs. Accessibility:* Does extreme mechanical difficulty alienate the very players who would appreciate the paper’s philosophical arguments, effectively turning narrative discovery into an elitist reward for twitch reflexes?
  5. *Silksong Protagonist Shift:* Does Hornet’s transition to a voiced character represent a mature evolution of storytelling, or does it dilute the eerie, open-ended interpretive magic that made the silent Knight so universally resonant?

## 2. Synthesis of Review and Author Rebuttal Strategy

The review panel unanimously commends the manuscript for its extraordinary scope, theoretical depth, and methodological rigor. The Devil's Advocate's challenges are vital and must not be brushed aside; rather, they must be incorporated directly into the manuscript's Discussion section as productive tensions that demonstrate scholarly self-awareness.

### Revision Action Plan:
1. **Integrate Devil’s Advocate Rebuttal:** Explicitly address the "iceberg illusion" critique by citing Team Cherry's documented development practices (ACMI 2022 interviews confirming that historical lore was drafted extensively before being fragmented).
2. **Clarify Epistemic Boundaries:** Re-verify that all claims regarding insect biology acknowledge the macroscopic poetic license of video game physics (e.g., ignoring the square-cube law).
3. **Refine Sacrificial Taxonomy:** Sharpen the conceptual distinction between involuntary eugenic sacrifice (Vessels) and voluntary mutual solidarity (Cloth, Quirrel, Sherma).
4. **Reinforce Canon Tiering:** Ensure that the unreleased status of *Sea of Sorrow* and the canon-agnostic nature of *Silksong*'s timeline are highlighted in all relevant matrices.

**Decision:** **ACCEPT WITH MINOR REVISIONS**  
The manuscript represents a landmark contribution to game studies, setting a new benchmark for interdisciplinary comparative video game research.
