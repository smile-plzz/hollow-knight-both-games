# Systematic Factions and Societies Register

## Overview and Source Basis
This document catalogs the political institutions, indigenous tribes, religious orders, and commercial guilds across *Hollow Knight* and *Hollow Knight: Silksong*. All records are synthesized from verified community encyclopedias and primary developer dispatches (`src_wiki_hk_lore`, `src_wiki_ss_characters`, `src_tc_blog_then_now`, `src_tc_blog_silksong_rev`).

---

## 1. Factions of Hallownest

### 1.1 The Pale Monarchy and the Royal Court
- **Dominion:** City of Tears, White Palace, Ancient Basin.
- **Ruler:** The Pale King and the White Lady.
- **Military / Champions:** The Five Great Knights (Ogrim, Isma, Ze'mer, Dryya, Hegemol) and the Kingsmould sentinels.
- **Ideology:** Sovereign order, expanded sapience through light, civilizational eternity ("Hallownest shall last eternal").
- **Collapse Mechanism:** The return of the Radiance's Infection broke the King's intellect, prompting the catastrophic Vessel program and eventual palace retreat into the Dream Realm.

### 1.2 The Scholars of the Soul Sanctum
- **Dominion:** Northern Spire of the City of Tears.
- **Leader:** Soul Master (Soul Tyrant).
- **Function:** Academy of natural science and soul research.
- **Ideology:** Technocratic immortality through the somatic harvesting of citizen Soul.
- **Atrocities:** Mass imprisonment, vivisection, and piling of corpses into sub-level bone pits.

### 1.3 The Mantis Tribe
- **Dominion:** Mantis Village in the Fungal Wastes.
- **Leaders:** The Mantis Lords (three sisters) and formerly the Traitor Lord.
- **Culture:** Proud territorial warriors governed by strict codes of honor, claw martial arts, and ancestral pride.
- **Political Status:** Autonomous non-aggression compact with the Pale King; held back Deepnest predators.
- **Schism:** The Traitor Lord embraced the Infection for strength and was exiled to Queen's Gardens with his loyalists.

### 1.4 The Moth Tribe
- **Dominion:** Resting Grounds.
- **Spiritual Origin:** Created by the Radiance; dedicated to dream reverence and cemetery care without weapons.
- **Historical Crisis:** Turned away from the Radiance to worship the Pale King's beacon (the Moth apostasy).
- **Fate:** Extinct by the time of the Knight's arrival, survived only by the repentant Seer who ascends into light.

### 1.5 The Kingdom of Beasts (Deepnest)
- **Dominion:** Deepnest and Distant Village.
- **Ruler:** Herrah the Beast.
- **Culture:** Matriarchal arachnid and centipede society rejecting the Pale King's intellect; savage, predatory darkness.
- **Alliance:** Herrah agreed to become a Dreamer on the sole condition of bearing the royal child Hornet.

### 1.6 The Hive
- **Dominion:** The Hive in Kingdom's Edge.
- **Ruler:** Queen Vespa (deceased) and Hive Knight.
- **Culture:** Golden bee colony organized around internal wax construction and royal devotion.
- **Ideology:** Radical isolationism; rejected the Pale King's beacon to protect their biological nature.

### 1.7 The Grimm Troupe
- **Dominion:** The Dream Realm / Nomadic Tents in Dirtmouth.
- **Master:** Troupe Master Grimm (Nightmare King Grimm).
- **Function:** Fire parasites feeding on the nightmares and ash of fallen kingdoms to sustain the Nightmare Heart.
- **Dissidence:** Brumm rebels against eternal servitude, seeking the player's aid to banish the troupe.

### 1.8 The Godseekers
- **Dominion:** Junk Pit / Godhome.
- **Leader:** Hive-minded Godseeker priestess.
- **Origin:** Fled the ruined Land of Storms.
- **Mission:** Attune to the gods of Hallownest using the Godtuner, inadvertently awakening the Void Given Form.

## 2. Factions of Pharloom

### 2.1 The Citadel Theocracy and Clergy
- **Dominion:** High Citadel, Gilded City, Choral Chambers.
- **Supreme Sovereign:** Grand Mother Silk.
- **Enforcers:** The Last Judge, Bell Wardens, Choir Masters, and Gilded Sentinels.
- **Ideology:** Acoustic salvation through divine song and upward pilgrimage, masking systemic somatic extraction.
- **Subjugation Mechanism:** Liturgical choral hymns, ringing bronze bells, and golden threads binding pilgrims into Puppet-Pilgrims.

### 2.2 The Ancestral Weaver Guild
- **Dominion:** Weavers' Cradle (Pharloom) and Weaver's Den (Deepnest).
- **Mythological Progenitor:** The First Weaver.
- **Key Figures:** Eva (the hermit seer), Weaver Elder, and Sister of the Loom.
- **Culture:** Sacred silk spinning, enchanted loomcraft, and independent guild secrets.
- **Historical Crisis:** Fought against the centralization of the Loom; dissident faction undertook the Great Migration to Hallownest.

### 2.3 The Pilgrims of the Song
- **Dominion:** The Great Ascent (Processional staircases from Moss Grotto to the High Citadel).
- **Representatives:** Sherma and wandering ascetics.
- **Condition:** Devout travelers seeking absolution, heavily exploited through Rosary fees and forced labor in the Marrow.

### 2.4 The Industrial Mining Guild (The Marrow)
- **Dominion:** The Marrow bone mines and blast furnaces.
- **Overseers:** The Iron Warden and bone smelters.
- **Laborers:** Enslaved pilgrims and debt workers (e.g. Drudge).
- **Product:** Pulverized bone, calcified chitin, and bronze bells for the Citadel.

### 2.5 The Commercial Guilds and Merchants
- **Dominion:** Gilded City bazaars and regional trade outposts.
- **Key Figures:** Gilded Merchant, Forge-Daughter (Deep Docks), Pinstress (Bonebottom).
- **Function:** Trade, needle forging, tool fabrication, and currency exchange utilizing Rosary beads.

### 2.6 The Flea Caravan
- **Dominion:** Mobile circus encampment in Bonebottom.
- **Leaders:** The Flea Master and Flea Cook.
- **Culture:** Acrobatic, cheerful fleas rescued by Hornet, creating a vibrant community haven amidst the ruins.

| **State Structure** | Autocratic Beacon Monarchy | Theocratic Liturgical Matriarchy |
| **Mode of Control** | Intellectual uplift via beacon light; eugenic containment | Acoustic hymns, bronze bells, and somatic thread binding |
| **Economic Basis** | Currency of Geo; feudal labor; royal tribute | Currency of Rosaries; forced debt labor; commercial bazaars |
| **Current Condition** | Dead necropolis; archaeological ruins; feral husks | Active living theocracy; functioning trade; active labor |
| **Resistance Mode** | Solitary deicide; awakening the Void to swallow the past | Grassroots solidarity; mutual aid Wishes; unweaving the loom |
