## 1. Summary of Source Content and Argument
ACMI documents Team Cherry's design history from early game jams (*Hungry Knight* at Ludum Dare 27, *Beneath the Surface* at Ludum Dare 29) to the sprawling world-building of Hallownest and Pharloom. The curatorial features highlight the studio's artisanal hand-drawn animation, environmental indexical storytelling, and the spatial transition from Hallownest's tight subterranean tunnels to the soaring vertical scale demanded by Hornet's agility.
