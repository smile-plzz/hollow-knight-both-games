## 1. Summary of Source Content and Argument
Caracciolo shifts focus from conventional video game world-building to "world disruption", analyzing how modern interactive media register contemporary ecological precarity and civilizational collapse. Examining titles including *Hollow Knight: Silksong*, the article analyzes apocalyptic and metareferential disruptions where ontological stability collapses, demonstrating how fragmented fictional worlds prompt players to negotiate crisis, mourning, and emergent community outside of monolithic state orders.
