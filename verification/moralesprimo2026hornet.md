## 1. Summary of Source Content and Argument
Morales-Primo evaluates protagonist Hornet's combat and traversal abilities through comparative arachnid biology. The paper shows that Hornet's *Silkspear* ability combines the hunting mechanisms of spitting spiders (spitting silk from distance) and bolas spiders (swinging a silk filament and reeling it back in via tensile strength). The *Thread Storm* ability reflects the multi-directional silk ejection of spitting spiders, while mid-air silk tethering mirrors aerial ballooning. Morales-Primo explicitly notes that while these abilities reflect genuine biological behaviors, they represent poetic, fictional adaptations rather than literal entomological simulation.
