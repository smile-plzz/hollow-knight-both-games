## 1. Summary of Source Content and Argument
Composer Christopher Larkin details his compositional philosophy for Team Cherry's games. He explains how the musical score avoids conventional bombastic action themes in favor of melancholy chamber instrumentation (piano, solo cello, viola), leitmotif recurrence, and dynamic spatial layers that react to the player's presence in abandoned civilizational ruins. He also discusses developing choral movements and brass themes to capture Pharloom's theocratic culture in *Silksong*.
