## 1. Summary of Source Content and Argument
Composer Christopher Larkin details his compositional philosophy across Hollow Knight and Silksong. He outlines how he avoids generic orchestral bombast in favor of chamber arrangements (piano, solo cello, viola), thematic leitmotif recurrence, and dynamic spatial layers that respond to subterranean isolation. He also discusses developing choral movements and brass themes to express Pharloom's theocratic culture in Silksong.
