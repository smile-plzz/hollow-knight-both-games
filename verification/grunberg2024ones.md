## 1. Summary of Source Content and Argument
Grunberg analyzes *Hollow Knight* through the philosophical framework of Ursula K. Le Guin's "The Ones Who Walk Away from Omelas." The paper argues that Hallownest’s imperial stability rests on a state-sanctioned utilitarian sacrifice: the perpetual agony and solitary imprisonment of a chosen child (the Hollow Knight) in the Black Egg to contain the Radiance. Crucially, Grunberg demonstrates that interactive digital games transcend literature's binary choice between complicity and walking away, granting players ergodic agency to confront, dismantle, or radically re-weave the sacrificial system across multiple branching endings.
