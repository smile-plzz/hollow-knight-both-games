## 1. Summary of Source Content and Argument
The paper examines the structural and ludonarrative function of backtracking in the Metroidvania genre. Rather than viewing backtracking merely as a functional gatekeeping mechanic to pad playtime, Zhang analyzes it as an indexical narrative mechanism. Revisiting previously cleared areas with newly unlocked abilities transforms familiar environments into dynamic narrative stages through altered enemy behaviors, evolving NPC dialogue, and unlocked historical indices.
