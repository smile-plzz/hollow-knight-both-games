## 1. Summary of Source Content and Argument
The paper examines the structural and ludonarrative function of backtracking in the Metroidvania genre. Rather than viewing backtracking merely as mechanical gating to extend playtime, Zhang analyzes it as an indexical narrative mechanism where revisiting previously cleared areas with newly acquired mobility reveals narrative transformations through changed NPC dialogues, environmental decay, and accessible lore tablets.
