## 1. Summary of Source Content and Argument
Jini Maxwell traces Team Cherry's game-jam roots from Ludum Dare 27 (Hungry Knight, August 2013) and Ludum Dare 29 (Beneath the Surface, April 2014) to Hollow Knight and Silksong. Maxwell documents that the world of Hallownest was only partly planned in advance, with world-building evolving iteratively alongside level design and expansion packs. Most Kickstarter stretch-goal areas were integrated into the base game, while some promised stretch goals (a third playable character) were dropped due to production realities.

## 3. Epistemic Assessment and Attribution Calibration
- Critical Correction Executed:
  - Earlier drafts attributed this article to ACMI corporate authorship in 2022 with a claim of extensive internal binders planning 90 percent of the lore in advance. The manuscript was revised to remove the unsupported 90 percent planning claim.
