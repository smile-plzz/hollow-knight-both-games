## 1. Verified Primary Records
1. **Kickstarter Campaign (November–December 2014):**
   - Goal: AU$ 35,000.
   - Total Raised: AU$ 57,129 (57,138 including final reconciliation) from 2,158 backers.
   - Stretch Goals Met: Additional areas, bosses, Colosseum of Fools, and the "Second Playable Character" (Hornet).
2. **Team Cherry Blog 2017 ("Then and Now"):**
   - Documents post-launch development, content roadmap, and the four free expansions (*Hidden Dreams*, *The Grimm Troupe*, *Lifeblood*, *Godmaster*).
3. **Team Cherry Blog 2019 ("Silksong Revealed"):**
   - Officially announces that the Hornet DLC evolved into a full standalone sequel: *Hollow Knight: Silksong*.
4. **Holiday 2025 Developer Update ("Sea of Sorrow"):**
   - Officially announces the upcoming free expansion *Sea of Sorrow*, detailing a nautical journey across and beneath the salt-stricken seas with new areas and bosses, slated for 2026.
   - Slated status confirmed unreleased as of 5 November 2026.
