## 1. Summary of Source Content and Argument
Wysocki provides a scholarly game review of *Hollow Knight: Silksong*, analyzing how the game deploys religious iconography, liturgical music, and architectural forms to structure player experience. Wysocki demonstrates how the theocracy of the Citadel converts economic and biological extraction into spiritual duty through the motif of the Pilgrimage, the ecclesiastical imagery of the "Last Judge" (wielding a thurible/incense burner), and the usage of rosary beads as currency.
