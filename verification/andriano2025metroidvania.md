## 1. Summary of Source Content and Argument
Andriano and Caracciolo investigate the environmental imagination of Metroidvania level design through *Hollow Knight* and *Rain World*. They argue that the genre's spatial backtracking, topological verticality, and environmental friction immerse players in nonhuman ecologies. The paper frames Hallownest not as a dead mechanical dungeon, but as a living ecological network where player traversal engages with multispecies entanglement, decay, and non-anthropocentric environments.
