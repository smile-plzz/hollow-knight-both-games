# Research Context: Hollow Knight & Hollow Knight: Silksong Comparative Study

- Conduct an interdisciplinary comparative study of Team Cherry's *Hollow Knight* (2017) and *Hollow Knight: Silksong* (2025).
- Target manuscript length: 10,000 to 13,000 words (excluding references and appendices).
- Formatting and Citations: APA 7th edition, backed by BibTeX (`references.bib`).
- Venue and Scope: Portfolio and hobby game research curated by Ismail, with strict evidence transparency.
- Maintain a three-tier canon certainty system (Confirmed Canon, Strongly Supported Interpretation, Speculative Theory).

## Constraints and Research Boundaries
