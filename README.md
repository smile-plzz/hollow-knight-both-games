# Beyond Hallownest: Civilization, Memory, Divinity, Ecology, and Identity in Hollow Knight and Hollow Knight: Silksong

An interdisciplinary academic comparative game studies research project examining Team Cherry's *Hollow Knight* (2017) and *Hollow Knight: Silksong* (2025).

## Overview
This project investigates how Team Cherry constructs meaningful fictional insect civilizations through interconnected geography, environmental storytelling, mythological structures, nonhuman ecological world-building, ludic mechanics, and fragmented historical knowledge.

## Project Structure
- `00_PROJECT_README.md` - Research overview, environment audit, and status
- `01_RESEARCH_BRIEF.md` - Research questions, objectives, theoretical framing, and scope

- `04_LITERATURE_SYNTHESIS.md` - Comprehensive literature review and research gap analysis
- `05_HOLLOW_KNIGHT_NARRATIVE.md` - Complete chronological and experiential narrative analysis of Hallownest
- `06_HOLLOW_KNIGHT_CHARACTER_ANALYSIS.md` - Master character census, individual profiles, and character matrix
- `07_HALLOWNEST_AREA_ANALYSIS.md` - Region-by-region spatial and environmental storytelling analysis
- `08_HOLLOW_KNIGHT_BOSSES_AND_EXPANSIONS.md` - Boss encounters, combat symbolism, and all 4 DLC expansions
- `09_HOLLOW_KNIGHT_ENDINGS.md` - All endings, philosophical dilemmas, and Omelas analysis

- `11_SILKSONG_CHARACTER_ANALYSIS.md` - Master character census, individual profiles, and Hornet/Lace analysis
- `12_PHARLOOM_AREA_ANALYSIS.md` - Region-by-region spatial and social architecture analysis of Pharloom
- `13_SILKSONG_BOSSES_AND_WISHES.md` - Boss encounters, Wishes system, and community restoration
- `14_SILKSONG_ENDINGS.md` - All available endings, Act structure, and thematic resolution
